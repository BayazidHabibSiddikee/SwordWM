  Can it replace i3wm — honest answer: not yet for a daily driver, but it's a real WM.

  Here's what SwordWM actually has vs. what i3 has:

  SwordWM has:

  - Master-stack tiling + monocle + float layouts
  - 9 workspaces with window movement
  - EWMH/ICCCM compliance (struts, window types, fullscreen)
  - Config file with live reload
  - Focus-follows-mouse
  - Gaps, title bars with Xft
  - Autostart

  Real gaps compared to i3:
